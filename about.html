⚡ The Challenge

Heavy Syllabus

A.L. subjects pack dense content, leaving little time to revise deeply before exams.

Studying Alone

Many students struggle with the same topics without realizing classmates could help.

Exam Pressure

Rising stress makes it harder to stay motivated and organized in the final stretch.

🎯 Our Objective

  • Create small, subject-focused peer groups for Grade 12 students
  • Turn revision into a shared, consistent habit rather than a last-minute scramble
  • Give struggling students access to peer support alongside teacher guidance
  • Build stronger exam results and lower stress across the batch

⚙️ How It Works

Group Size

4–6 students per group, mixed by subject stream

Frequency

Two sessions per week, after school, 60–90 minutes

Format

Rotate a “lead” each week who prepares a topic summary + practice questions

Teacher Support

A subject teacher checks in every fortnight

α vs Ω — Why Two Teams?

Manageable Size

Two mid-sized teams are easier to coordinate than one large batch.

Friendly Competition

A bit of rivalry between Alpha and Omega keeps energy high.

Shared Ownership

Each team can run its own streams without waiting on everyone.

Easier to Track

Teachers can monitor progress and give feedback more easily.

👥 Team Structure

💡 Why It Will Help

Better Understanding

Explaining topics to peers deepens your own grasp.

Accountability

A scheduled group session is harder to skip than solo revision.

Shared Resources

Notes and past papers get pooled instead of duplicated.

Lower Stress

Facing exams with friends feels far less overwhelming.
