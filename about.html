Grade 12 Study Group Initiative

Learning together, achieving more — a peer-driven plan to help Grade 12 students prepare for their exams.

The Challenge

Why Grade 12 needs a better way to prepare

!

Heavy syllabus

A.L. subjects pack in dense content, leaving little time to revise deeply before exams.

?

Studying alone

Many students struggle silently with the same topics, without realizing classmates could help.

×

Exam pressure

Rising stress makes it harder to stay motivated and organized in the final stretch.

Our Objective

Turn revision into a shared habit

  • 01Create small, subject-focused peer groups for Grade 12 students.
  • 02Turn revision into a shared, consistent habit rather than a last-minute scramble.
  • 03Give struggling students access to peer support alongside teacher guidance.
  • 04Build stronger exam results and lower stress across the batch.
